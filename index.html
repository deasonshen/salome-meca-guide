Salome-Meca Remote User Guide

只需 4 步，从自己的电脑使用服务器上的 Salome-Meca。

Use Salome-Meca on the server from your own computer in 4 steps.

  1. 1

    下载安装 TurboVNC Viewer

    打开 TurboVNC 官方下载页面，下载并安装与你的电脑（Windows / macOS / Linux）相符的 Viewer。

  2. 2

    连接自己的远程桌面

    打开 TurboVNC Viewer，在服务器地址栏输入你对应的地址，然后点击 Connect。

    sjtu08111.186.37.47::5928

    注意：地址中是两个英文冒号 ::。

  3. 3

    输入密码，启动 Salome-Meca

    输入老师提供的 VNC 密码，进入 XFCE 桌面。双击桌面的 Salome-Meca 2021 图标即可打开软件。

    如双击无法打开，可打开桌面终端，输入 start-salome-meca

  4. 4

    保存文件并退出

    把工程文件保存在自己的个人文件夹中。使用结束后，保存工作，直接关闭本地 TurboVNC Viewer 窗口即可断开连接。

    不需要在 XFCE 中选择“注销”。如只是断开 Viewer，原来的远程桌面通常会保留。

小提示：找不到最小化的 Salome-Meca？按 Alt + Tab 切换窗口。无法连接或密码错误？核对自己的地址后联系老师。
  1. 1

    Install TurboVNC Viewer

    Open the official TurboVNC download page. Download and install the Viewer for your computer (Windows / macOS / Linux).

  2. 2

    Connect to your remote desktop

    Open TurboVNC Viewer, enter your assigned server address, and click Connect.

    sjtu08111.186.37.47::5928

    Note: The address contains two colons (::).

  3. 3

    Enter your password and launch Salome-Meca

    Enter the VNC password provided by your instructor. After the XFCE desktop appears, double-click Salome-Meca 2021.

    If the shortcut does not work, open Terminal and enter start-salome-meca

  4. 4

    Save your work and disconnect

    Save your project files in your own home folder. When finished, save your work and simply close the TurboVNC Viewer window on your computer.

    You do not need to log out of XFCE. Disconnecting the Viewer normally leaves your remote desktop running.

Tip: Can't find a minimized Salome-Meca window? Press Alt + Tab. Can't connect or have a password issue? Double-check your assigned address and contact your instructor.
